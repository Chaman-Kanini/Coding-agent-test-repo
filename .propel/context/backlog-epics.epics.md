## Epic Descriptions

### EP-001: Ticket intake

**Business Value**: Every customer problem reaches the team as one ticket with what an agent needs to start.

**Description**: Customers and agents open tickets, attach files and see a ticket state, from the web or by email.

**Priority**: High

**Mapped Requirements**: N/A

**UI Impact**: Yes

**Key Deliverables**:
- Ticket API
- New ticket form
- Attachment upload
- Email to ticket

**Dependent EPICs**: N/A

### EP-002: Agent workspace

**Business Value**: Agents clear tickets faster because the queue, the ticket and the reply tools are in one place.

**Description**: Agents work a queue, take tickets, reply, add private notes and hand tickets on.

**Priority**: High

**Mapped Requirements**: N/A

**UI Impact**: Yes

**Key Deliverables**:
- Ticket queue
- Assignment
- Reply and notes
- Canned replies

**Dependent EPICs**: N/A

### EP-003: Customer portal

**Business Value**: Customers solve common problems without a ticket and track the ones they open.

**Description**: A portal where customers sign in, list their tickets, search help articles and rate an answer.

**Priority**: Medium

**Mapped Requirements**: N/A

**UI Impact**: Yes

**Key Deliverables**:
- Sign in
- My tickets
- Help articles
- Satisfaction rating

**Dependent EPICs**: N/A

### EP-004: SLA and notifications

**Business Value**: No ticket waits unseen: the clock is visible and people are told before it runs out.

**Description**: Service level timers per priority, warnings before a breach, and email and in app notices.

**Priority**: High

**Mapped Requirements**: N/A

**UI Impact**: Yes

**Key Deliverables**:
- SLA policies
- Timers
- Breach warnings
- Notification centre

**Dependent EPICs**: N/A

**Priority**: Medium

**Mapped Requirements**: N/A
